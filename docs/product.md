# Veyro Product Direction

## Initial problem

Shopping research is fragmented. Users describe a need in natural language, while product information is spread across brands, retailers and review sources.

## Example

> I need comfortable walking shoes under ₹10,000. I have a wide foot and want something suitable for daily walking.

Veyro should identify the hard constraints, research candidate shoes, compare evidence and explain why a product made the shortlist.

## Initial brands

Nike, Puma, ASICS, Crocs, Skechers and other relevant footwear brands/retailers can be added through providers.

## Product principle

Veyro should not behave like a simple product-search box. The value is in doing the research and making the comparison understandable.
